# Share: Traffic Light Protocol (TLP 2.0)

> **In one sentence:** a colour label on each piece that says who may receive it.

## The labels

| Label | Who may receive it |
|-------|--------------------|
| ![TLP:RED](../../assets/badges-tlp/tlp-red.svg) | Named recipients only, no further sharing |
| ![TLP:AMBER+STRICT](../../assets/badges-tlp/tlp-amber-strict.svg) | The recipient's organisation only |
| ![TLP:AMBER](../../assets/badges-tlp/tlp-amber.svg) | The recipient's organisation, and those who need to know to act on it |
| ![TLP:GREEN](../../assets/badges-tlp/tlp-green.svg) | The community, not public channels |
| ![TLP:CLEAR](../../assets/badges-tlp/tlp-clear.svg) | Anyone |

Defined by FIRST: https://www.first.org/tlp/

## Rules of thumb

- **Label every piece and every document.** Unlabelled means nobody knows who may see it.
- **The label travels with the piece**, not with the person. If you forward it, the label comes with it.
- **When in doubt, pick the stricter one.** You can relax a label later; you cannot un-share.
- The person who creates the piece sets the label. Only they can relax it.

## Where to write it

- In the [custody log](share-custody-log.md), on the line of the piece.
- In the file name if useful: `group2_..._TLP-AMBER.har`.
- Said aloud when you hand something over.

## In the lab

What label would a real HAR get, holding a real person's session and file? Decide as a group, write it, and be ready to say why.
